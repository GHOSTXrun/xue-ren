How it works

Small push. Big ball.

SNOWBALL turns a coin's creator fees into a steady stream of small token drops to active Solana traders who don't hold it yet. Recipients don't connect, register or claim anything. Each new holder can bring trades, trades bring fees, and fees roll the next, bigger round. That's the snowball effect.

02The mechanics

Step by step

01
Register your coin

Paste the pump.fun contract address. SNOWBALL verifies on-chain that it's a SOL-paired pump.fun coin with no freeze authority, reads its name and logo, and creates a dedicated fee wallet. Its key is generated and encrypted on the server and never shown to anyone.

02
Give it a push: route your creator fees

Add the fee wallet as a shareholder in pump.fun's creator-fee sharing, or send SOL to it directly. Only the coin's creator can do either. SNOWBALL can't redirect fees on its own. The first 0.02 SOL becomes the wallet's operating reserve for network fees.

03
The ball rolls: SNOWBALL buys your coin

Every finalized deposit is booked once. Network costs come off first, then 100% of the net goes to holder growth. When that budget reaches 0.05 SOL, a round buys your coin on its bonding curve, or through Jupiter after graduation. Every buy is simulated first and capped for slippage and price impact.

04
It picks up flakes: drops to new wallets

Bought tokens are split equally between active traders that don't hold your coin, about $0.001 each. A wallet can receive a given coin only once, which a database constraint enforces, so every round reaches only fresh snow. Every batch is confirmed on-chain and checked recipient by recipient before it's recorded.

05
It rolls bigger: the loop repeats

New holders are new eyes on the chart. Trading creates creator fees, fees fund the next round, and the next round reaches new wallets again. Funds that are too small for an economical round wait until they are enough, like snow piling up before the next push.

06
Every fee goes into the ball

100% of net fees buy the coin and drop it to active traders. No buyback, no burn: every lamport works for holder growth, and every buy and drop is on Solscan.

Definitions

Who counts as a flake

A flake is an active trader wallet in SNOWBALL's index. The discovery engine reads recent pump.fun bonding-curve and PumpSwap transactions from chain and records who traded. A wallet is eligible when, in the last 240 hours, it:

  • ❄ made at least 2 buys or sells across at least 1 coin(s)
  • ❄ is a plain wallet, not a program, PDA or pool account
  • ❄ stayed under 3000 trades and 25 trades in any single minute (bot heuristics)
  • ❄ isn't on the exclusion list (exchanges, LPs, known bots, opted-out wallets)

Classification is probabilistic. Eligible means the wallet behaves like an active trader, not that a human is behind it.

What SNOWBALL does not promise

  • ❄ No trading volume, price movement or permanent holders.
  • ❄ Wallets reached ≠ holders. “Retained” counts recipients that still hold today.
  • ❄ Coverage is measured against SNOWBALL's own observed index, never all of Solana.
  • ❄ A new token account costs ~0.002 SOL in rent, far more than a $0.001 gift. That cost is shown, never hidden.
