SNOWBALL sends small amounts of registered coins to wallets that look like active traders. If you don't want them, sign a message with your wallet (no transaction, no fees). Your wallet will be excluded from every future round for every coin.

snowball

SNOWBALL buys registered coins with their creator fees and sends small amounts to wallets that look like active traders, based on what this index has observed on-chain. Wallet classification is probabilistic. An airdrop is not a purchase or an endorsement by the recipient. Reaching a wallet does not mean it keeps holding, and the snowball effect is a mechanism, not a guarantee. Nothing here is a promise of volume, price or holders, and nothing here is financial advice.
